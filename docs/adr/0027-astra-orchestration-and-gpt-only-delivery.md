# Astra orchestration and GPT-only delivery

- Date: 2026-09-08
- Approved by: user, explicit plan-upgrade and reassignment instruction
- Decision owner: Astra

Astra owns system-wide design, contracts, canon decisions and final integration. Sol provides bounded complex design/counter-review. Terra owns subsystem/content implementation and tooling. Luna owns independent adversarial QA, review and verification.

All Ollama outsourcing is recalled. Kimi implementation/dialogue and MiniMax fixture/tool/content work transfer to Terra; GLM failure modes, coverage gaps and consistency review transfer to Luna. No new Ollama calls, probes, retries or scheduled monitoring. Preserve existing artifacts, media and historical acceptance records.

This supersedes staffing and future utilization obligations in older contracts, not game behavior, REQ/AC IDs, allowlists or approval history. Existing Sol approvals remain valid; future final approval/escalation is Astra. Each new unit records actual GPT participation and separate implementation/verification ownership.

Astra Medium standard is the default. Pro-mode escalation is conditional on confirmed execution support. Current subagent controls expose model and effort only; higher effort must not be reported as Pro. Do not invent IDs or introduce separately billed APIs. Sol is not a mandatory extra serial review on routine units.
