# OpenAI image-output terms basis — 2026-09-20

- Checked: 2026-09-20, Asia/Seoul
- Official source: https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/
- Source publication/effective date shown by the page: 2026-01-01
- Relevant section: `Content`, especially ownership and input-responsibility paragraphs

The official terms state, in relevant part, that the user owns Output as between the user and OpenAI, subject to applicable law. They also place responsibility on the user to have the rights, licences and permissions needed for Input. The terms additionally require human evaluation of output appropriateness and prohibit infringing use.

This note supports only the OpenAI-to-user output-rights leg. It does not prove that every historical reference input was owned or licensed, does not identify the exact historical image model/version, and is not a legal opinion. The project must separately preserve the reference-image chain and obtain the user's project-use/modification attestation before accepting A1/H1 media.
